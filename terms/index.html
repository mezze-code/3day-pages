3Day — Terms & Community Rules

Last updated: August 26, 2026

These are the terms you agree to when you create a 3Day account, and the community rules the app asks you to accept before your first group-chat message. They're short on purpose.

The community rules

  • Chat is for the group. Everyone on your 3Day sees what you post. Content you share belongs in front of the people you're sharing a weekend with.
  • No harassment or hate. Zero tolerance. No abusive, threatening, hateful, sexually explicit, or otherwise objectionable content — this is someone's weekend.
  • Report anything off. Long-press any message to report it. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and content that breaks these rules is removed. You can also block any member instantly — their content disappears for you immediately and permanently.

Your account

You sign in with Sign in with Apple and must be at least 13 years old. You're responsible for what you post. We may suspend or remove accounts that break the community rules, and we may remove content that violates them. You can delete your account at any time (Profile → Delete account) — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what deletion does.

Your content

What you share on a 3Day stays yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to the other members of that 3Day — that's the whole product, and the only thing we use it for. No ads, no tracking, no selling data.

Changes
