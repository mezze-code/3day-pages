3Day — Support

Need a hand with 3Day? Start with the FAQ below. If you're still stuck, email us at 3day@gmail.com and we'll help.

Frequently asked questions

What is 3Day?

3Day is a home for your multi-day events — festivals, reunions, weddings, retreats, and long weekends away. Instead of scattering a trip across a group chat, a spreadsheet, and five different apps, 3Day gives the whole event one hub: a page for every day, a shared agenda, rides and adventures, group chat, and a commemorative ticket you can keep in Apple Wallet.

How do I create a 3Day?

From the Hub, tap Create a 3Day (you'll also see it on the first-run screen when you don't have one yet). The wizard walks you through four quick steps — the feeling, the picture, the days, and a review — and when you launch it, your 3Day is saved to your account so everyone you invite sees the same weekend. Hosts can edit the details, build the day-by-day agenda, and manage rides and adventures from the Manage button on the Hub.

How do I invite people to my 3Day?

Every 3Day has a 6-character invite code, shown on the host's Hub. Tap it to copy it, or use the share button to send it to your group. Anyone with the code can join your 3Day.

How do I join with an invite code?

Sign in, then tap Join with an invite code on the Hub (or "I already have an invite" on the welcome screen), and enter the 6-character code from your host. You'll land in their 3Day and see the agenda, chat, and everything else the group shares.

How does group chat stay safe?

Before your first message, 3Day shows the community rules. In chat, long-press any message to report it — reports are reviewed within 24 hours — or to block its author, which hides their content for you immediately and permanently. You can also reach us any time at 3day@gmail.com about anything in your group.

How do I add my ticket to Apple Wallet?

Open your event, go to the Ticket (keepsake) screen, and tap Add to Apple Wallet. The commemorative pass is handed off to Apple's Wallet app, where you can keep it alongside your other passes. You can also flip the ticket between its QR face and photo face by swiping or tapping the dots.

How do I try the app without setting anything up?

From the Hub's empty state, tap "See the demo weekend" to explore a fully-featured example 3Day. It's clearly labeled DEMO so you always know it's sample content, not your own.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account, profile, messages, and RSVPs from our servers, revokes 3Day's Sign in with Apple access, and wipes the app's data on your device. If you host a 3Day that others have joined, the 3Day itself passes to the earliest-joined member so their weekend isn't destroyed; a 3Day with no other members is deleted outright. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where is my data stored?

Your account and the content you share with your 3Day are stored on our servers so everyone in the group sees the same weekend. A copy of your own content is also cached on your device so the app opens instantly and works offline. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what we collect — and what we don't (no analytics, no ads, no tracking).

Do I need an account?

To create or join a real 3Day, yes — sign in with Sign in with Apple, which we use only for authentication (hiding your email is fully supported). You can explore the demo weekend without an account.
