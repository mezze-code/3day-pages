Frequently asked questions

What is 3Day?

3Day is a home for your multi-day events — festivals, reunions, weddings, retreats, and long weekends away. Instead of scattering a trip across a group chat, a spreadsheet, and five different apps, 3Day gives the whole event one hub: a page for every day, a shared agenda, flight and ride coordination, group chat, and a commemorative ticket you can keep in Apple Wallet.

How do I create an event?

From the Hub, tap Create a 3Day (you'll also see it on the first-run screen when you don't have an event yet). The create wizard walks you through it step by step: the vibe, the big picture, how many days, who's coming, getting there, and shared spaces. Review the summary and your event is saved on your device.

How do I add my ticket to Apple Wallet?

Open your event, go to the Ticket (keepsake) screen, and tap Add to Apple Wallet. The commemorative pass is handed off to Apple's Wallet app, where you can keep it alongside your other passes. You can also flip the ticket between its QR face and photo face by swiping or tapping the dots.

How do I try the app without setting anything up?

On the welcome screen, tap "I already have an invite" (or "See the demo weekend") to explore a fully-featured example event. It's clearly labeled DEMO so you always know it's sample content, not your own.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm. This removes your profile, events, and ticket data from your device and signs you out. In version 1.0, everything is stored locally on your device, so deletion is immediate and complete.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. 3Day version 1.0 does not collect or transmit your data — your events, profile, chat, and ticket live on-device. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use 3Day as a guest. Sign in with Apple is available if you'd like a signed-in identity, and it's used only for authentication.
