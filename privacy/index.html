3Day — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

3Day keeps a group's weekend in one place — which means the things you share with your group are stored on our servers so your group can see them. This policy explains exactly what we collect and why.

The short version

We collect only what the app needs to work: your account (name, username, email, a user ID) and the content you share with your 3Day (the plans you build, chat messages, RSVPs). We run no analytics or ads, use no tracking, and never sell or share your data with anyone.

What we collect

  • Your account — your display name, username, avatar emoji, email address (or Apple's private relay address if you hide it), and an account identifier. Used to sign you in and to show who's who inside a 3Day.
  • Your 3Day content — the 3Days you create or join: titles, dates, day plans and events, rides, adventures, and your RSVPs. Shared with the other members of that 3Day, and no one else.
  • Chat messages — what you post to a 3Day's group chat, visible to that group. Reports and blocks you file are also stored so we can act on them.

What we don't do

  • We do not run analytics or tracking, and we use no advertising identifiers.
  • We do not include third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for anything except making 3Day work.

Where your data lives

Your account and shared content are stored on our backend (hosted on Cloudflare) so everyone in your 3Day sees the same weekend. A copy of your own content is also cached on your device so the app opens instantly and works offline.

Sign in with Apple

3Day uses Sign in with Apple to authenticate you. We verify your Apple identity token, store your account identifier, and keep the credential needed to revoke our access when you delete your account. We never see your Apple password, and hiding your email through Apple's private relay is fully supported.

Community safety

Group chat carries community rules you agree to before your first message. You can report any message (we review reports within 24 hours) and block any member (their content disappears for you immediately and stays gone). Contact us any time at the address below.

Deleting your account and data

Delete your account any time, directly in the app: Profile → Delete account.

This deletes your account, profile, messages, RSVPs, and photos from our servers, revokes 3Day's Sign in with Apple access, and wipes the app's data on your device. One exception: if you host a 3Day that other people have joined, the 3Day itself survives — ownership passes to the earliest-joined member so their weekend isn't destroyed with your account. A 3Day with no other members is deleted outright.

Apple Wallet

If you add a commemorative ticket to Apple Wallet, that pass is handled by Apple's Wallet app under Apple's own privacy terms. 3Day does not receive any information back from Wallet.

Children's privacy

3Day is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of 3Day changes what we collect (for example, shared photo uploads), we will update this policy before that version ships and describe exactly what changed.
