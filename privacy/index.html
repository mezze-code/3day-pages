3Day — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

3Day is a local-first app. This policy explains exactly what happens to your information when you use 3Day version 1.0.

The short version

3Day does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you create in 3Day — your events, your profile, your day agendas, your chat, and your ticket — is stored only on your device. There is no 3Day server that receives your content.

What we collect

Nothing. 3Day version 1.0 does not collect or transmit any data to us or to any third party.

  • We do not run analytics or tracking.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not include third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not have a backend that stores your content.

Where your data lives

All of the data you create in 3Day — including your name and avatar, the events you build, day-by-day agendas, flight and ride notes, group chat messages, and your commemorative ticket — is stored only on your device. It never leaves your device through 3Day.

If you delete the app, that on-device data is removed by the operating system along with it.

Sign in with Apple

3Day offers Sign in with Apple solely to authenticate you (to establish a signed-in identity on your device). We use it only for authentication. We do not use it to build a profile of you, and we do not transmit your Apple credentials or identity to any 3Day server. If you choose to hide your email through Apple's private relay, that is fully supported.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and erase your on-device data at any time, directly in the app:

Profile → Delete account.

This removes your stored profile and event data from the device and signs you out. Because version 1.0 stores everything locally, deletion is immediate and complete on the device.

Apple Wallet

If you add a commemorative ticket to Apple Wallet, that pass is handled by Apple's Wallet app under Apple's own privacy terms. 3Day does not receive any information back from Wallet.

Children's privacy

3Day is not directed to children under 13, and because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of 3Day introduces features that collect or transmit data (for example, a cloud sync backend), we will update this policy before that version ships and describe exactly what changes.
